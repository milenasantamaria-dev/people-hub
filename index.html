Our Story

Supply chain planning hasn't kept pace with the world. Companies are still running on systems built for a slower, more predictable era. The result: excess inventory, missed service levels, and planning cycles that grind instead of decide.

Daybreak builds AI labor for supply chain. Systems that own decisions, not just suggest them. Demand, inventory, replenishment: the routine calls run inside defined bounds and are measured on outcomes. People handle the exceptions.

We combine AI, behavioral science, and deep supply chain expertise. Companies that work with us carry less inventory, hit higher service levels, and plan faster without adding headcount.

Our WOW

Five Ways of Working that turn our F.O.C.U.S. values into action.

🤝One Daybreak
F · First Principle Thinking

We act as one company, not a stack of teams. Outcomes don't belong to silos.

  • Share context across teams first
  • Optimize for Daybreak, not your team
  • Hand off the why, not just the what
🤖Build AI first. Work AI first.
O · Optimized Sustainability

AI is the default, in what we build and how we work.

  • Design with AI in the workflow
  • Use AI in your own daily work
  • Learn new tools before you need them
📈Compound, don't coast
C · Continuous Growth

We choose the work that compounds, for us, our team, and the company.

  • Take the work that levels you up
  • Reflect on what worked and didn't
  • Build reusable systems, not heroics
🎯Own the outcome
U · Ultimate Value

We commit to the result, not the activity. We stay accountable until the value lands.

  • Name what "done well" looks like
  • Raise flags early when at risk
  • Carry it past the handoff
🗣️Say it straight
S · Simplified Execution

We say the hard thing kindly, the first time. Disagree in the room, commit once decided.

  • Honest status updates, not hopeful ones
  • Real time feedback, not saved for reviews
  • Disagree in the meeting, commit after

🎁 Benefits

Open a location to see the benefits available to Breakers based there.

🇺🇸 United States
Health & Insurance Benefits
  • Medical, vision, and dental insurance (managed via PayChex/BeneAdmin)
  • FSA (Flexible Spending Account) — see 2026 contribution limits below
  • HSA (Health Savings Account): for employees enrolled in an HDHP with no active FSA
  • 401(k) retirement plan (Empower): automatically enrolled at 5% self-contribution
  • Short-term/long-term disability and life insurance — automatically enrolled
  • Commuter Benefits (transportation)
  • Annual Benefits Open Enrollment
FSA — 2026 Contribution Limits
Medical FSA:annual limit $3,300; up to $670 can roll over into 2026
Dependent Care FSA:$5,000 per household; $2,500 if married and filing separately
Compensation & Payroll
  • Multi-state taxation: withholding based on Resident State vs. Work State, with a 10-day non-resident threshold
  • Overtime pay (FLSA): over 40 hrs/week; in California, over 8 hrs/day up to 12, and the first 8 hrs on the 7th consecutive workday — no comp time in lieu of overtime
  • Phone & internet stipend: $50/month for VPs and above, and Breakers in Recruiting, Sales (Account Executives, Business Development), or Professional Services · $35/month for all other Breakers
Time Off
  • Flexible Time Off (FTO): no fixed limit, doesn't accrue, not paid out at separation
  • 12 paid sick days per calendar year
  • Maternity and parental leave
  • Paid company holidays per the US Holiday Calendar

🇮🇳 India
Health & Insurance Benefits
  • Health insurance sum insured: ₹20,00,000
  • Accidental cover: ₹50,00,000
  • Maternity coverage: ₹1,50,000
  • Family coverage includes up to 2 parents/parents-in-law
Compensation & Payroll
  • PF (Provident Fund): 12% on basic salary
Time Off
  • Flexible Time Off (FTO): no fixed day limit, for vacation/leisure/personal time; doesn't accrue and isn't paid out on separation
  • Probationary PTO: 3 days during the probation period (expires at confirmation, not encashed)
  • Paid Holidays: 12 company-declared holidays per year (see the 2026 India Holidays calendar)
  • Paid Sick Leave: up to 12 days/year, carryover capped at 5 days, account balance capped at 17 days total
  • Maternity Leave: up to 26 weeks (max 8 weeks before expected delivery) for the first two children; 12 weeks for a 3rd child or for adoption of a child under 3 months; 45 days paid leave in case of miscarriage
  • Paternity Leave: 10 working days, to be used within 30 days of the child's birth
  • Bereavement/Funeral Leave: 2 weeks for immediate family, 5 days for non-immediate family
  • Personal Leave of Absence (Leave Without Pay/LWP): available for personal matters, civic service, or work-related studies, subject to manager and People Team approval
